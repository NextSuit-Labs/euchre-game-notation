NextSuit Labs

Building Open Infrastructure for
Trick-Taking Card Games

NextSuit Labs is an independent, developer-led project crafting open data standards, creator tools, and provably fair cryptographic protocols for online card play — starting with the best one: Euchre.

Explore The Three Pillars View Our Roadmap

Our Mission

To create software infrastructure that powers cross-platform integration, fosters vibrant community engagement, and establishes uncompromising game integrity.

Chess built an open digital empire on universal notation (PGN), rich spectator tooling, and transparent evaluation engines. Trick-taking games deserve that same foundation. NextSuit Labs develops the open connective tissue that unites players, club directors, content creators, and card room managers.

The Three Pillars

Our technical focus is divided into three pillars spanning open standards, creator tooling, and verifiable cryptography.

PILLAR 01

Standardized Notation & Integration

Creating open, deterministic standards that decouple match facts from proprietary apps, ensuring player records remain open, portable, and permanently owned by the community.

  • Deterministic Minimalism: Store only uncomputable decisions (dealer, up-card, bids, plays) and let parsers construct the exact state tree.
  • Open Data Sovereignty: Hand histories and tournament statistics remain portable across mobile, web, and physical games—free from closed app siloes.
  • Dual Serialization: Human-readable JSON schemas alongside bitpacked binary streams for high-speed tournament databases.
  • Open Source Engine: TypeScript parsers, converter CLI tools, and validator libraries published under Apache 2.0.
Live Standard (v1.5)
PILLAR 02

Creator, League & Tournament Tools

Building software to power engaging media, tournament operations, and organized Euchre league play for streamers, clubs, and directors.

  • Interactive Replayers & GIF Exporters: Step through tricks, branch decision trees, inspect mistake evaluations, and export animated match highlights.
  • Live Match Logging & Scoring: Rapid keyboard and touch-optimized scorekeeping for instant EGN and multi-game EMN generation.
  • League & Tournament Operations: Series scheduling, table rotations, transparent Elo tracking, and dispute resolution audits.
  • Broadcast Overlays: Real-time Canvas2D graphic injectors for tournament live streams and Twitch/YouTube broadcasts.
Live Apps & Active Prototypes
PILLAR 03

Online Security & Provable Trust

Replacing algorithmic skepticism with mathematical certainty through verifiable dealing oracles, collusion telemetry, and deterministic rule verifiers.

  • Provably Fair Shuffling: CSPRNG hash-commitments and multi-party entropy injection to mathematically guarantee untampered decks.
  • Zero-Knowledge Deals: Commutative encryption protocols for serverless Mental Poker play without centralized deal leaks.
  • Anti-Collusion & Rule Audits: Automated signaling detection, illegal play prevention, and deterministic state verifiers to eliminate disputes.
